For Anisha Jogee, public service has always been about one thing: making the community she calls home even stronger.

An Arlington Heights resident, mother, small business owner, former educator, and longtime community advocate, Anisha has spent years listening to residents, bringing people together, and serving the community since 2017.

Now, she's running for Village Trustee to put that experience to work for all of Arlington Heights.
